Modes

A mode changes how Claude helps and which shortcut buttons you see, for activities like homework, practice, writing or coding. Switch modes from the chat bar, or by right-clicking the top widget. Modes never change your privacy settings or what needs your approval.

Claude & privacy

Everything below stays on this PC. Claude only sees it when you ask for a recap or a task scan.

Screen memory
Notes which app you're in and keeps screenshots from the last ~30 minutes, for "What did I just do?" and finding tasks.
Chat speed
Fast answers instantly; Smart thinks harder on tough questions. Replies stream in either way.

Every 40 minutes, looks through recent activity and changed notes for things you need to do. Uses some of your Claude usage.
