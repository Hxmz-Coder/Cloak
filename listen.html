Listen

Let the AI listen along

Listen turns whatever is playing on your computer, like a class, a meeting, a call or a video, into text, and the AI responds to it.

  • It answers questions as they're asked, and points out deadlines and instructions.
  • It adds assignments it hears to your Tasks.
  • You can ask it anything about what was said.

Speech is turned into text right here on your PC, so the audio never leaves it. Setting up downloads the speech engine once (about 500 MB).

Listens to your computer's sound, not your microphone.
